# Custom Music Files (4 Switchable Songs)

To play your own favorite songs on the website:

1. Put your MP3 files into this folder (`public/music/`):
   - `song1.mp3` (or `favorite.mp3`)
   - `song2.mp3`
   - `song3.mp3`
   - `song4.mp3`

2. When you visit the site, clicking the **Play** button starts the music, and clicking the **Next** button switches to the next custom song in the queue (or choose directly from the playlist popup)!
